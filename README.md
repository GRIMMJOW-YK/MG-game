## 🎮 About the Game

**Gandhi Bhai — The Salt March Runner** is a fast-paced, single-file HTML5 canvas game. You play as a runner inspired by the historic Dandi March of 1930, dodging obstacles, collecting salt crystals and charkhas (spinning wheels), and building up a powerful Ultimate meter.

No frameworks. No build tools. Just pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

**Author:** Yadhu Krishna

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏃 **Endless runner gameplay** with increasing speed and difficulty
- 🧂 **Collectibles**: Salt crystals, Charkhas (bonus coins)
- 🛡️ **Power-ups**: Shield, Magnet, Boost, Slow-Mo, 2× Score
- ⚡ **Ultimate System**: Fill the meter to trigger a transformation
- 👤 **4 Playable Characters** with unique stats and abilities
- 🏆 **Local Leaderboard** (top 30 scores, saved in browser)
- 🎯 **4 Difficulty Levels**: Easy, Normal, Hard, Insane
- 🎨 **Dynamic sky** that changes during Ultimate mode
- 🔊 **Web Audio API** sound effects (no external files)
- 📱 **Mobile-friendly** with touch controls and fullscreen support
- 💾 **Persistent player profile** (name + best score)
- 🎬 **Animated character sprites** drawn entirely with Canvas 2D
